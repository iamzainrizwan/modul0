The first version of this site was a fake terminal I hand-wrote in vanilla JavaScript over a couple of weekends. It was fun to build, but fairly miserable to use if you just wanted to know what I'd worked on.

This version is a site that's pretty, represents my (current) visual language, with this blog attached. My old terminal still lives here - `` ` `` opens it. Type `eggs` and see if you can find them all ;) The site also obeys Vim keybinds and some others - press `?` to see them all. No I will not be adding emacs. 

## Why a Site?
Honestly, I just wanted a place to talk about my work that felt like myself. Instagram felt too angled outward, LinkedIn was so corporate I think I would've started talking to my friends in terms of the top 10 ways to drive engagement or some bullshit, and I had honestly always wanted to make a website. It also just... kinda made sense for a CS student to be able to host a simple single-writer blog, and I had a lot of fun figuring out hosting and storage while trying to keep it (mostly) free. Got there in the end, and learnt about Cloudflare's D1 along the way which was fun :)
